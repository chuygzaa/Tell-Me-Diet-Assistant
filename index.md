# Privacy Policy for Fitness Assistant

_Last updated: [june 12, 2026]_

Fitness Assistant ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial project developed by an individual for personal use and limited testing. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## Summary
- Your nutrition logs, weight entries, and profile details are stored **locally on your device**.
- The app has **no user accounts**, shows **no ads**, and uses **no third-party analytics or tracking**.
- The only data that leaves your device is the **text describing a meal you log**, which is sent to the app's backend to be interpreted (see "Meal processing" below).

## Information stored on your device
The app stores the following locally, in a database on your device:
- **Meal logs:** the foods you log, their quantities, and computed nutrition values.
- **Weight entries:** weights and dates you record.
- **Profile details:** the name and sex you enter during setup, used to personalize goals and nutrient targets.

This information stays on your device. It is not uploaded to any account.

## Meal processing (data that leaves your device)
When you log a meal by voice or text, the **text describing that meal** (for example, "8 oz of steak and a banana") is sent to the app's private backend server so it can be interpreted into specific foods and quantities. To do this, the backend uses **Google's Gemini API**, which processes the text according to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Only the meal description text is sent for this purpose. Your weight entries and profile details are **not** transmitted. The app does not send your data to any other third party.

## Voice input
If you use voice entry, your device's speech recognition converts your speech to text. That conversion is handled by your device and operating system, subject to your device maker's privacy terms. The app receives only the resulting text.

## What the app does not do
- It does not create accounts or collect login credentials.
- It does not display advertising.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
- It does not sell, rent, or share your personal information.
- It does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Data retention and deletion
Because your data is stored locally, you control it. **Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data** from your device. There is no separate account to delete.

## Security
Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security measures. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. This is a personal project provided on a best-effort basis, without warranty.

## Changes to this policy
This policy may be updated as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent change.
